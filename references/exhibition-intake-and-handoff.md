# Exhibition, Booth, and Spatial Projects: Inputs and Handoff

## Minimum inputs before strategy or outline

1. Project objective, key audience, success criteria, and key reception/use cases.
2. Organization, product, technology, or business materials and publication boundaries.
3. Brand guide, source logo files, typography, and existing visual assets.
4. CAD/floor plan, dimensions, height, site photographs, MEP, fire, load-bearing, and other site constraints.
5. Area, budget, schedule, procurement/approval path, and delivery scope.
6. VIP or key-guest types, headcount, duration, route, confidentiality, and security requirements.
7. Existing content, video, cases, data, exhibits, and their rights or approval status.

Do not lock a theme or technical solution when these inputs are missing. State which judgement cannot yet be made.

## Inputs after strategy or outline

For each zone or content module, record its message, needed evidence, assets, rights, update cadence, reviewer, display interface, and required date. For interactive, film, or digital content, add deployment, API, network, account, and operations responsibilities.

## Project-start handoff card

- Confirmed client objectives and reception tasks.
- Confirmed audiences, duration, route, and site conditions.
- Evidence available and evidence gaps.
- Team research questions and value propositions to investigate.
- Open content, spatial, media, and operations decisions.
- Assumptions prohibited until client confirmation.

This card starts the next specialist workflow. It is not a theme, narrative, or design conclusion.
