## Cross-industry input categories

Choose only inputs relevant to the project. Do not ask for every item at once.

| Category | Typical input | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and goals | Background, decision context, goals, success criteria, budget, schedule, scope | Priority and boundaries |
| Subject matter | Organization, products/services, evidence, case material, technical claims | Content and value judgement |
| Brand and content assets | Brand guide, source logos, font rights, image/video/copy, rights status | Available assets and expression constraints |
| People and approvals | Audiences, decision-makers, users, reviewers, collaborators | Information hierarchy and decision path |
| Environment and systems | Drawings, dimensions, existing systems, interfaces, network, safety/regulatory, maintenance | Delivery and experience conditions |
| Delivery governance | Deliverables, version process, acceptance, confidentiality, dates, budget rules | Responsibilities and project boundaries |

## Two-round input gathering

Before strategy or outline, gather what is needed to judge goals, audiences, subject matter, current conditions, decision paths, and hard constraints.

After strategy or outline, collect module-specific assets, data, rights, interfaces, update owners, and approvers. Do not portray an unresolved creative option as a basic missing input.
