# Anonymous example: a fragmented launch brief

This example is fictional. It demonstrates source boundaries and decision logic, not a real project, client, budget, venue, or transcript.

## Materials received

- **[Client brief]** “Create an in-person launch experience that helps priority guests understand the new service.”
- **[Meeting input]** “A working launch month has been mentioned, but it is not yet confirmed.”
- **[Internal working draft]** “A guided route could be considered.” This is a proposal, not a client requirement.
- **[Client confirmation needed]** Audience size, venue conditions, budget range, approval owner, available product evidence, and the intended definition of success.

## Diagnosis

### Confirmed requirements

- The project is an in-person launch experience. [Client brief]
- A priority guest group is important. [Client brief]
- The experience needs to make a new service understandable. [Client brief]

### First-round input readiness

| Item | Status | Why it matters | Recommended owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guest types and approximate headcount | Needed | Determines journey, content depth, and capacity assumptions | Client |
| Venue drawing, measurements, and site restrictions | Needed | Determines what experience formats are feasible | Client / venue |
| Product facts, claims, and demonstrable evidence | Needed | Determines what can be communicated credibly | Client |
| Budget range and procurement route | Needed | Determines scope and delivery options | Client |
| Decision maker and approval checkpoints | Needed | Determines when the next-stage proposal can be reviewed | Client |
| Experience route options | Team work item | A proposal must follow the confirmed inputs above | Team |

### Focused confirmation questions

1. Which guests must leave with what understanding or action?
2. Is the proposed launch month a fixed deadline, a target, or an early estimate?
3. Which venue constraints are already known, and who can confirm them?
4. Which service claims, demonstrations, images, or data are approved for use?
5. Who gives final approval for scope, creative direction, and budget?

### Next-action card

- **Client:** provide the available product, audience, venue, and approval information.
- **Team:** prepare a project-start diagnosis and, once the inputs are available, propose experience routes.
- **Condition to enter strategy:** objective, audience, available evidence, site conditions, decision path, and hard timing constraints have been confirmed or explicitly recorded as assumptions.
